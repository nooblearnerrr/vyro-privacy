VYRO – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

VYRO is a media player for Windows made by Nasru. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

What VYRO collects

Nothing. VYRO has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer does not receive any data about you or how you use the app.

What stays on your PC

Your settings, watch history (so videos can resume where you left off), add-on list and IPTV provider details are saved only on your computer, in your Windows user profile. Passwords and API keys are encrypted with Windows' built-in data protection for your user account. Uninstalling VYRO removes them.

Services VYRO connects to

VYRO only connects to online services when you use a feature that needs them:

  • Add-ons and IPTV providers you add yourself – VYRO requests catalogs, titles and streams from the addresses you enter. Those services have their own privacy policies.
  • Cinemeta (Stremio's public catalog) – for searching titles and showing posters.
  • OpenSubtitles – only if you search or download subtitles, using the key/account you enter.
  • Watch party – messages (play/pause/seek and chat) are encrypted on your PC with a key made from the party code and passed through a public message relay. The relay cannot read them.

When VYRO connects to a service, that service can see your IP address, as with any internet connection.

Children

VYRO is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.
